# B-071: The paper's CROW benchmark cannot be reproduced from the repo
severity: low | category: physics | effort: S | fix-class: needs-human | verdict: CONFIRMED | status: partly: the paper CROW (random lengths) is generated by tests/bench/crow_gen.py (moved and tested in 095d46c, PF06), which also reproduces crow_tb (--preset crow_tb, checked by tests/bench/test_oracles.py); crow_tb (`-t ac_crow`) removed in sprint 2026-10c PK (decision "Before v0.4.0"), its circuit is the generator's preset; CROW class unchanged; Fig. 3 timings not republished (decision of 2026-10-23)

## Problem
The ECOC paper benchmarks a CROW with 2N waveguides whose lengths are L_i ~ N(33.2, 5.0) µm, κ = 0.15 (power), N up to 512, and 10 pulses of 1 mW / 1 ns at 500 MHz.
- `CROW::connect_submodules()` gives every waveguide the same length `m_ring_length/2` (crow.cpp:28). It has no per-waveguide lengths and no random variation, and the variation code is commented out (:20-21, :27, :30).
- crow_tb uses different values: L ≈ 776 µm per ring, through power 0.8, neff = 1, ng = 2, N = 3 (crow_tb.cpp:179-190, overriding values set two lines earlier).
## Evidence
Code at the cited lines. The paper is discover/paper-zrounba2023-specs-ecoc.txt (Results §).
## Expected
A way to reproduce Fig. 3, for example per-waveguide lengths plus a seeded normal sampler, and a tb or netlist with the paper's parameters.
## Suggested fix
A human decides whether reproducing the paper is a goal. If so, add `setWaveguideLengths(vector<double>)` or a seeded variation option, and a `crow_paper` tb.
## Golden impact
None, unless tb/ac_crow parameters are changed.

## Update (sprint 2026-10c, PF06)
`tests/bench/crow_gen.py` writes the paper's CROW as a netlist (2N waveguides with seeded N(33.2, 5) um lengths,
kp = 0.15, 10 pulses of 1 mW / 1 ns at 500 MHz; any N), and `--preset crow_tb` the testbench's circuit; the
oracle suite checks it against closed forms and against `specs -t ac_crow` (same event times, powers within
1e-12 W). The `CROW` class still gives identical halves: random lengths there would change `tb/ac_crow`,
and the netlist route makes it unnecessary for the paper. The decision of 2026-10-23 keeps `crow_tb` and does not
republish the paper's numbers before a fixed benchmark machine exists; what remains is that decision (delete
`crow_tb` now that the generator covers it, and when to publish regenerated Fig. 3 numbers).

## Update (sprint 2026-10c, PK)
The maintainer decided the deletion ("Before v0.4.0", 2026-10-23): `src/tb/crow_tb.cpp`, `-t ac_crow` and
`--nrings_crow` are removed; the golden cases `regress/crow-tb-preset-n3` and `regress/crow-tb-preset-n8-vci`
run the generator's `crow_tb` preset instead. What remains open: when to publish regenerated Fig. 3 numbers.
